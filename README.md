### 🧭 About Me

I'm a Principal Software Engineer with over 10 years of hands-on experience in full-stack development, system architecture, and automation. I currently lead automation and system architecture initiatives for a nationwide enterprise system at the **Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB)**, where I was promoted from Senior Software Engineer to Principal Software Engineer.

My background spans large-scale government and enterprise systems — including the Computerization of Police Stations (CPS) project across Pakistan — where I've designed scalable backend architectures, optimized databases at scale, integrated critical third-party APIs, and mentored engineering teams on modern web development standards. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Sargodha.

I care about clean architecture, measurable performance, and building systems that hold up under real-world load.

### 🧠 Engineering Philosophy

I believe good software is judged by how it behaves after launch, not on the day of it. That means clean, maintainable architecture over clever shortcuts; performance and scalability designed in from the start, not patched on later; and automation wherever a manual process can be trusted to a system instead. I invest in mentoring engineers because standards compound — a well-mentored team ships better systems than any single expert working alone. I stay hands-on with new tools and frameworks deliberately, because the best solution to a problem is rarely the one I already know.
